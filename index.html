Four steps, from payment to receipt

Every payment follows the same connected path to a fiscal receipt.

1

Create and connect

Register your business and connect your M-Pesa channel and KRA eTIMS/OSCU.

2

Order and pay

A customer places an order and pays through your M-Pesa PayBill.

3

Match and fiscalize

The payment is matched to its order and sent to KRA through your eTIMS integration.

4

Receipt delivered

The customer gets an SMS with a link to their digital receipt.
