Reference / Design system

One palette, two themes

Every component below is the real thing, styled from tokens.css. Use the moon in the top bar to switch themes.

Colour tokens.css

Semantic names, not hex values. Each one is defined once for light and redefined for dark, so a component rule never asks which theme is showing. Rebranding means editing the ramp at the top of the file.

Type Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter

Plus Jakarta Sans sets headings and figures; Inter carries body text and table data. Money and counts use tabular figures so columns line up as values change.

Layout breakpoints

Three steps, each one chosen because something actually breaks at that width rather than to match a device.

  • > 1200pxSidebar docked. Charts sit two-up beside each other.
  • ≤ 1200pxChart and connection pairs drop to one column; the hero illustration is dropped rather than squeezed.
  • ≤ 1000pxSidebar leaves the grid and becomes a drawer behind the burger, over a scrim. Escape or a tap outside closes it, and choosing a destination closes it too.
  • ≤ 600pxSingle column, condensed padding, full-width filters, notifications pinned to the screen edges. Inputs stay at 14px so iOS does not zoom on focus.
  • ≤ 420pxSearch collapses out of the top bar; the theme and bell buttons keep their place.
Try it: narrow this window past 1000px and the sidebar becomes a drawer. The theme follows your device until you press the moon, and that choice is then remembered.
